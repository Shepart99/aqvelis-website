§ 2 Description of services

AQVELIS is a currently free service for managing water environments: pools, spas (hot tubs), freshwater aquariums and saltwater aquariums. Using the app, you can:

  • create and manage one or more water environments with name, facility type and volume,
  • record measurements for your water environments,
  • set personal target ranges for individual measurement parameters,
  • assign products from the AQVELIS product catalog to your water environments,
  • view dosing notes for the assigned products based on curated manufacturer information.

A data field is provided for measurement devices; active synchronization with external sensors or measurement devices does not currently take place.

§ 8 Deleting your account

You can delete your AQVELIS account yourself at any time. You can find the corresponding menu item in the app under:

Settings → Danger Zone → “Delete Account”

After selecting “Delete Account”, you confirm the process in two steps; finally, you confirm with “Delete Account Permanently”. In the process, your profile, your legal consents, your water environments/facilities, measurement target ranges, measurements, product assignments to your facilities and measurement device metadata are completely deleted. Global product catalog data remains, as it is not assigned to you personally.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request the deletion of your account by email to support@aqvelis.at.
