Smart Water Management

AQVELIS

Smart Water Management for Pool & Aquarium

AQVELIS is an app for smart water management. It helps to record water parameters, document measurements and keep track of multiple pools and aquariums in one place.

The AQVELIS app is currently in a testing phase. A public release is in preparation.

Features

What AQVELIS aims to offer

AQVELIS is being developed for managing water environments – from recording individual measurements to keeping track of multiple basins.

Manage water parameters clearly

Collect relevant water parameters in one place instead of manually keeping notes and individual measurements in your head.

Document measurements

Record measurement results and follow the development of water quality over time.

Support for water care & dosing

AQVELIS aims to help plan and document care steps and dosing in a traceable way.

Manage multiple water environments

Create multiple pools and aquariums in parallel and keep track of each of them separately.

Notices as a product goal

In the long term, AQVELIS aims to use notices to help you keep important water parameters in view.

Planned
Areas

For pool and aquarium

AQVELIS covers two water environments with different requirements for care and measurements.

Pool

Whether a private pool or a pool facility: AQVELIS aims to help keep track of water parameters and make ongoing care clearer.

Aquarium

For freshwater and saltwater aquariums: AQVELIS aims to support documenting water parameters and tracking them over longer periods.
